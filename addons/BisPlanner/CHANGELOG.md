## 1.1.0
- Plans no longer pick gear that isn't in Forever: Season of Discovery items (the Five Thunders, Feralheart and other tier 0.5 epics), level-30 copies of raid items such as Thunderfury and Sulfuras, and developer items ([TMP], OLD, Hidden, Template) are gone from the list.
- Plans no longer pick another class's set: every tier 3 piece is its own class's (a shaman got Cryptstalker, a mage Plagueheart), and so are dungeon sets 1 and 2. Each Atiesh is for its own class.
- New option, Armor: "Your class's own" (to begin with) uses only the heaviest armor your class wears: plate for warriors and paladins from level 40, mail for hunters and shamans from 40. "Any you can wear" brings back the rest, such as leather on a warrior.
- Forever's "Premier" PvP sets count as PvP gear, so they stay out while PvP gear is off.
- Your settings were saved twice for a character when the realm name wasn't known yet; the two are joined.

## 1.0.0
- First version. Give each stat a weight, set goals ("hit at least 30"), and the planner picks one item per slot so the whole set reaches every goal and scores as high as it can.
- The plan is worked out once and saved; it is planned again only when a weight, a goal, a filter or your level changes.
- Plans for your own level and follows you as you level, or for any level you type.
- Each row says where the item comes from when the game's tables know: profession, faction and standing, PvP rank, bind on equip. Questie adds quests, drops and vendors when it is installed.
- Hover a row for the next best items. Right-click leaves an item out; shift-click links it.
- Several named sets of weights, shared by your characters.
- Options: best quality, crafted, reputation and PvP gear, two-handed or one-handed weapons, off-hand weapons.
- A line on item tooltips: in your plan, or how it scores against the plan's item.
- This is a new addon: the game finds a new addon folder only when it starts, so close the game fully and start it again once.
