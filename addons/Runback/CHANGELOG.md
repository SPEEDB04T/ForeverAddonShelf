# Changelog

## 1.1.0

- The readout and the timer bars can be minimized. A small button at the right of the readout (the game's own quest tracker collapse button) hides the bars and shrinks the readout to one line, "Runback" and the soonest countdown; click it again to restore. Also `/runback min`, and a "Minimized" tick box in the options. Kept over a /reload; the timers keep running while minimized, and dragging still works.

## 1.0.0

- First version. A readout with the nearest graveyard, its distance and the run back from it, for you and for enemy players.
- Timer bars for enemy players who die near you (from the release when it is seen, otherwise the earliest they can be back), for group members who release, and for your own run back.
- Graveyards the list does not have are learned from where you land when you release; ghost speed is measured on your own ghost.
