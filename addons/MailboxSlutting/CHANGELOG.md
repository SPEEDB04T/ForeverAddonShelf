# Changelog

## 1.9.1

- Five lines at nobody are out, because they could be read as sexual content in public chat: "starts dancing a little dirtier.", "grins at a dirty thought.", "could keep this up all night.", "enjoys this a little too much." and "takes it nice and slow." Eight are left for now. No line the addon sends has a word like "dirty" in it any more.

## 1.9.0

- **The lines at nobody no longer invent onlookers.** Half of them spoke of "whoever is watching" or "somebody staring" whether or not anybody was. Real onlookers already get an emote at their name, so the lines at nobody are now about the mood, the face and the dance, and cheekier. The thirteen: "puts a little more hip into it.", "tries to look innocent.", "starts dancing a little dirtier.", "regrets nothing.", "takes it nice and slow.", "grins at a dirty thought.", "gives up on being subtle.", "could keep this up all night.", "has no plans to behave.", "gets a little carried away.", "enjoys this a little too much.", "looks far too pleased about something.", "was raised better than this."
- **A line about people is only said while it is true.** Seven are left ("catches somebody staring and winks.", "stares right back at somebody.", "knows exactly who is watching.", "gives the crowd something to look at.", "makes sure everybody gets a good look.", "lets everybody stare.", "smirks at somebody who has been staring a while."). Each needs somebody who really has you targeted; until 1.8.0 most of them were also said on a guess, and always with nameplates off. With nobody looking, or nameplates off, you get the lines about nobody only.
- **At most every fourth line is about people**, however many are staring. Skip on such a line brings up one about nobody.
- Gone: "dances a little slower for whoever is watching.", "grins at whoever is staring.", "dances just out of reach.", "enjoys being watched.", "pretends not to notice the staring.", "smirks at the crowd.", "checks out somebody in the crowd."
- **Tip jar**: "gives the tip jar a wink." in place of "winks at whoever tipped today."

## 1.8.0

- **The next line is on the counter.** Under "Next line" you see what Say a line will say, before the click. **Skip** passes on it and brings up another; nothing is said. A tip jar line that is due is shown the same way, and Skip drops it.
- **Lines that fit the crowd.** The addon counts who is in view and who has you targeted (nameplates shown). A line about "the crowd" or "everybody" waits until three people are in view; the lines about staring come up sooner while somebody is. Two new lines: "lets everybody stare." (only while three or more have you targeted) and "smirks at somebody who has been staring a while." (only while somebody has for half a minute). With nameplates off it knows nothing and uses the 14 plain lines as before.
- **Somebody who dances at you gets a smile or a grin.** Until now they got nothing.
- **The answers by itself take turns.** A wink is answered with a wink most of the time, now and then with a smirk, a grin or a giggle; the same for the other emotes, each within its own kind. The same person never gets the same one twice running, and the same emote does not go out three times in a row.
- **Lookers, less like clockwork.** The wink came after exactly three seconds for everybody. Now it is a wink, a smile or a smirk after 3 to 7 seconds of looking, picked anew for every look, and about one look in four gets nothing.
- **Somebody who was rude twice gets nothing more** until you log out: no second pout, no button on their row, no answer to whatever they do after it.
- **The list says how long somebody has looked** ("is looking at you, 45s", "looked at you for 2m 05s"), and of those looking right now, whoever has looked longest is on top.
- **The jar count names today's biggest tip**, when it was a gold or more: "counts the tip jar: 9 tips today. The biggest was 20 gold pieces."
- **Quick whispers.** A whisperer's row has a Reply button: a menu with "hey <3", "glad you like it <3", "dancing for tips <3", "keep watching <3" and the emote. Nothing is whispered without your click.

## 1.7.0

- A line at nobody goes out only when you click **Say a line** (or **Tip jar**) on the counter. Key presses no longer send one, so walking, jumping or typing says nothing. The button still lights up when a line is due.
- An answer to somebody's emote comes 2 to 5 seconds later, at a random moment each time, where it came in the same instant. The wink at somebody who keeps you targeted comes half a second to 2.5 seconds after the three seconds. Nothing goes out if you answered with a click in the meantime, went AFK or stopped dancing, or if the looker looked away.
- No mailbox anywhere: the addon's description and two messages said "mailbox"; they don't any more. Nothing it sends ever did, and nothing in it looks for one. Only the name is left.
- Every line and reaction was put in front of three hostile reviewers. What changed:
  - **Lines at nobody: 14, one idea each, where 22 held about 7 ideas** (nine were "smiles, winks or grins at the crowd", four were "enjoys being watched"). New: "stares right back at somebody.", "starts dancing a little dirtier.", "checks out somebody in the crowd.", "makes sure everybody gets a good look.", "grins at whoever is staring." Gone, among the repeats: "does not mind the staring at all.", "lets the crowd look.", "grins at the ones staring.", "eyes somebody in the crowd."
  - **What goes back by itself**: a kiss gets a kiss (it was a blush), a whistle a sly smirk (a curtsey), a lick a purr (a giggle), a purr a purr and "sexy" a flirt (both a wink), a cheer a grin ("thanks Bob."), a hug a giggle (a hug back; Hug is still on the button).
  - **Rude emotes get a pout**, where it was "cries on Bob's shoulder." Sigh and Cry are still on the button.
  - **Off the buttons**: Act bashful, Shy, Nod, Blink, Chuckle, Greet, Say hello, Welcome, Bow, Thank, Cuddle, Frown and Spin. Gone with them: "smiles warmly at Bob." and "blushes and looks away from Bob."
  - **Tip jar**: "winks at whoever tipped today." in place of "leaves the tip jar where everybody can reach it."
  - **Welcome back**: "gives Bob a wink for coming back." and "smiles to see Bob back." in place of "waves at Bob again.", "smiles at Bob again." and "greets Bob warmly."
  - **Thank-yous**: a gold or more gets "gives Bob a wink for the tip of..." (it was the same "thanks" as a few silver), and 20 gold or more "blows Bob a slow kiss for the tip of..." in place of the bow.
  - **Whispers**: "thank you <3", "ty <3" or "thanks <3".

## 1.6.0

- The dance is sent again every 30 to 60 seconds, at a random moment each time. Somebody who came into view after you started saw you standing still; now they see the dance within a minute. It waits while you walk, fight or are AFK, and leaves you alone if you do another emote yourself (until you dance again).
- A click in the world no longer sets off a line or brings the dance back, so clicking back into the game's window says nothing. Lines by itself go out on your next key press only. The buttons on the counter work as before.
- The dance the addon sends does not count as "another emote of yours", so it does not hold the lines at nobody back.

## 1.5.3

- Fewer lines at nobody: one every 30 to 60 seconds, where it was one every 10 to 30. The first comes 20 to 40 seconds after you start.
- A line at nobody never goes out within 30 seconds of another emote of yours (an answer, a thank-you, a welcome back, one you typed). One that is waiting for a key press waits a little longer.
- The welcome back uses the whole name: "welcomes Googi Baba back.", where it said "welcomes Googi back."

## 1.5.2

- The lines at nobody are plainer, the way players type an emote. Gone: "licks a lip and looks over the crowd.", "bites a lip and keeps dancing.", "sways those hips a little slower.", "runs a slow look over the crowd.", "takes the next step slow, for the view." and the like. In: "winks at the crowd.", "enjoys the attention.", "grins at the ones staring.", "eyes somebody in the crowd."
- The tip jar line with the toe is gone; two tip jar lines are left.

## 1.5.1

- "glances over a shoulder at the crowd." is gone: somebody in the dance animation does not turn and look back. In its place: "takes it slow for the ones staring." The giggle "behind a hand" is now "gives Bob a little giggle.", since the hands are busy dancing.

## 1.5.0

- Works wherever you dance, not only on a mailbox. No line says where you stand any more: "smiles down at Bob.", "gives the crowd below something to look at." and the like are reworded ("gives Bob a slow smile.", "gives the crowd something to look at."). Nothing else ever needed a mailbox.
- Text can no longer run over the buttons. A long emote ("Blaize smirks at you from all the way across...") is cut short with "..." above the big button and in its row, and every text in the counter (the line above the big button, the rows, the hint under them, the status line, today's numbers) now has a fixed box: one line, or two for the hint and the status line, and nothing drawn outside it.

## 1.4.2

- Less at one person. A tipper gets the thank-you and the whisper, and no "dances for Bob." after it.
- The "glances at the tip jar, then down at Bob." line is gone: nobody is pointed at the tip jar any more, least of all somebody who just tipped.

## 1.4.1

- Four lines that tried to be clever are out (the ones about checking the mail, forgetting the mail, pretending to check it, and the tip jar speaking for itself). Plain ones in their place: "smiles at whoever is looking up.", "runs a slow look over the crowd.", "dances with eyes on somebody in the crowd."

## 1.4.0

- The lines at nobody are rewritten to tease instead of wait: somebody who knows they are being watched ("dances a little slower for whoever is watching.", "catches somebody staring and smiles.", "makes checking the mail take longer than it should."). Gone are the flat ones (keeps dancing, hums a tune) and the needy ones (looks around for an audience).
- Far less about the tip jar: a line every 8 to 12 minutes instead of every 2 to 3, the first after 3 to 5 minutes, and the four that are left do not look at the jar or hope for anything.

## 1.3.3

- Somebody who only targets you gets the game's wink by itself again, as before 1.3.2.
- The dance resumes right away after any emote by itself: about a second later instead of 3 to 7, and if it does not take it is tried again every 2.5 seconds, up to 8 times. The 15 seconds that used to be kept between two dances no longer hold the comeback back.

## 1.3.2

- The dance holds better. Somebody who only targets you no longer gets the game's own wink, which stopped your dance each time: the wink goes in /e on your next key press or click (the dance goes on), and is dropped after 10 seconds without one.
- After an emote that does stop the dance (an answer to somebody who emoted at you), the dance is tried again every 4 seconds, up to 6 times, instead of once more after 15 seconds. A try that does not take shows nothing, so this does not spam.
- A key press or click also brings the dance back when it is down.
- A dance that did not take no longer counts toward "the game does not let an addon emote here".

## 1.3.1

- The lines at nobody are rewritten for what people actually see: somebody dancing nonstop on a mailbox. No more poses, spins, bows, waves, hops or stretches that the character does not do; the 22 lines are looks, smiles, humming and the dance going on ("looks over the crowd without missing a step.").
- The tip jar lines likewise: a glance, a nod or a toe, no holding the jar out, counting coins or polishing it.

## 1.3.0

- New big button **Say a line**: one click says the next line at nobody, right away. It is the big button while nobody is waiting for an answer, and sits small at the right of the row when somebody is. It lights up while a line is waiting for your key press or click.

## 1.2.2

- The game lets a /e line from an addon out only on your own key press or click, and says "action failed because of an addon" otherwise. So the lines by itself (at nobody, the tip jar, the glance at the tip jar, the dance for a tipper) now wait for your next key press or click in the world and go out on that. Any key counts: a step, a jump, a modifier.
- Nothing is sent in /e from a timer or from somebody's chat line any more, so that message should not come up again. Answers by itself use the game's own emotes (wink, smile), which are allowed; your clicks still use the /e wording.
- One line waits at a time, so a burst of keys does not send a burst of lines. Not at the keyboard, no lines.

## 1.2.1

- The lines by itself (at nobody, the tip jar, the glance at the tip jar, the dance for a tipper) did not go out: the game drops a /e line sent from a timer. They now wait for the next chat line or emote from anybody around and go out on that. In a city that is a few seconds at most. With no chat at all for 15 seconds the line is tried from the timer anyway.
- A line at nobody that still did not show is sent once more on the next chat line.

## 1.2.0

- Much louder when the crowd does nothing: while people are around and nobody is emoting at you, whispering or tipping, a line at nobody goes out every 10 to 30 seconds (it was every 7 to 13 minutes), and a tip jar line every 2 to 3 minutes (it was every 20 to 30).
- 24 lines at nobody instead of 5, and 12 tip jar lines instead of 7, so they do not come round too soon.
- When somebody does emote at you, whisper or tip, these lines wait 45 seconds, so the answer to them is what people read.

## 1.1.0

- Nothing of Slutmog Shop is in the window any more, with or without the shop installed: no ad row, no Post ad or Reroll, no Wares or Options button, no Whisper price, and its name on top is Mailbox Slutting. The shop reminds you of its ads in its own window again.
- The button reads Start dancing / Stop dancing, and the texts no longer talk about a shop.
- The numbers no longer count ads.
- A whisperer on the big button is answered with an emote, like everybody else.

## 1.0.0

- Works without Slutmog Shop: the counter then has no ad part, no Wares or Options button, and no Whisper price.
- With Slutmog Shop 1.19.0 or newer the counter is as before: the shop's next ad, Post ad, Reroll, Wares, Options and Whisper price.

- Key bindings of its own: Accept a tip, and Start or stop dancing. The Accept tip key bound under Slutmog Shop keeps working.
- This is a new addon folder: the game needs one full restart to see it, a /reload is not enough.
