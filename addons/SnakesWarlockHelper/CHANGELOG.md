# Changelog

## 1.1.0 (2026-11-14)

- Minimap button on warlocks: left-click opens or closes the window, right-click opens the Options tab, drag moves it. A tick on the Options tab and `/swh minimap` hide it.
- Options > AddOns has a Snakes Warlock Helper page that opens the Options tab.
- In the addon list next to the minimap, a right-click opens the Options tab.

## 1.0.0 (2026-11-14)

First version.

- Reminder icons for a missing demon, Soul Link, Demon Armor / Demon Skin and the weapon stone. Click to cast out of combat; right-click to quiet for 10 minutes.
- Demon comparison window: each demon's own damage plus what it adds to your spells, Demonic Sacrifice, and a one-line verdict.
- Options tab, `/swh` commands and a `/swh demo` mode that works on any class.
