## 1.0.1
- Dungeon, raid, group and elite quests are on the route again: a finished quest goes where its turn-in NPC stands, whatever its tag (the Deadmines quests are handed in at Sentinel Hill, not inside). Their row shows the quest log's tag ("Dungeon", "Group", "Elite").
- Only quests handed in inside a dungeon or raid are left off the route, in a list called "Hand in inside a dungeon" that names the dungeon. A cave the game draws as a dungeon map but that lies in the open world stays on the route.

## 1.0.0
- New addon: the hand-in route that used to be part of Abandon Advisor. Abandon Advisor now only advises what to drop.
- Plans a real route instead of nearest-by-yards. It goes zone by zone and town by town, with the flight paths this character knows, the hearthstone (when it will be ready), and the boats, zeppelins and tram your faction can ride. The trip is shown as numbered legs ("1. Hearth to Goldshire (3), 2 min") with a time for each leg.
- The Follow route button, a click on a leg, or `/turnin next` point the game's quest arrow at the next stop. It moves on by itself as you hand quests in.
- Group, dungeon and raid quests, and quests with no known turn-in place, are listed under the route instead of on it.
- Quest log tab with ForeverCore (otherwise a side button), options window and minimap button.
