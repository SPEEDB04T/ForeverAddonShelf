Installing by hand

  1. Download an addon (or all of them). You get a .zip.
  2. Unzip it into your WoW Forever AddOns folder so each addon keeps its own folder:
    World of Warcraft\_forever_\Interface\AddOns
  3. Restart the game, or log out to character select, and tick the addon in the AddOns list.
  4. To update, download again and overwrite the old folder. Your settings are kept.
  5. Using WowUp? Choose "Install from URL" and paste the addon's GitHub link, and it will update by itself.
